Cold Chain & Quality Control

From harvest pre-cooling to computer-monitored refrigerated containers across maritime routes.

2H POST-HARVEST

Rapid Pre-Cooling

Crops undergo forced-air hydrocooling and rapid room pre-cooling within 2 hours of field picking to eliminate field heat and lock in shelf life.

BRIX & CALIBER

Optical Grading & Sizing

Automated sorting lines inspect skin blemishes, brix sweetness levels, and millimeter-exact caliber distribution prior to export packaging.

24/7 SATELLITE

IoT Reefer Tracking

Every 40ft High Cube Reefer container is outfitted with satellite temperature, humidity, and gas monitors for 24/7 telemetry until discharge.
